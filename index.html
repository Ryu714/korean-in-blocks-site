Korean in Blocks

Watch Hangul snap together, one block at a time.

Korean in Blocks is a YouTube channel of short animations that build Korean words from the consonants and vowels of Hangul.

Watch: youtube.com/@KoreaninBlocks

Korean in Blocks Tools

Korean in Blocks Tools is a private helper used only by the channel owner to read and reply to comments on the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users.
